Who We Are

About Us

TenderCare Surrogacy Agency is a professional surrogacy and fertility support agency dedicated to helping intended parents build families safely, legally, and ethically. We offer structured guidance, medical coordination, legal documentation support, and emotional care for both intended parents and surrogate mothers.

Founder & CEO

Meet Folashade

Folashade is the founder of TenderCare Surrogacy, bringing expertise, compassion, and unwavering commitment to every surrogacy journey.

With hands-on experience in the surrogacy field since 2022, her passion for connecting intended parents with the right surrogate mothers is matched by her dedication to guidance, confidentiality, and support at every stage.

She works closely with trusted medical and legal partners, ensuring that every intended parent and surrogate is treated with care, dignity, and clear communication from start to finish.
